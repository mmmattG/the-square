Version: 0.1.4
Date: 2026-07-25
  Features:
    - Added a handcraftable bootstrap recipe for hand-grown pentapod eggs on Gleba, based on feedback from @Birk.
    - Added a handcraftable bootstrap recipe for hand-grown biter eggs on Nauvis.
  Balancing:
    - Removed Nauvis biter egg ingress and bioflux egress in favour of handcrafted biter eggs to better fit with vanilla captive biter spawners.
  Bugfixes:
    - Base screenshots now capture the supported planet currently being viewed, based on feedback from @Birk.
    - Crude oil ingress is now unlocked by Oil gathering instead of Oil processing, based on reports from Redlight and seb_zeppelin on the Mod Portal.
  Credits:
    - Thanks to @Birk on Discord for feedback
    - Thanks to @Redlight and seb_zeppelin for feedback on Mod Portal

Version: 0.1.3
Date: 2026-05-27
  Features:
    - Added Space Age support for planets, Vulcanus, Fulgora, Gleba and Aquilo.
    - Added a per-player "Get cliff explosives" mod setting button to help recover from cliff generation blocking progression on Vulcanus.
  Changes:
    - This is a major update, with entire systems reworked to better fit Space Age.
    - Reworked the ingress/egress system around craftable anchors.
    - Removed the expansion point purchasing system. Ingress and egress anchors are now crafted and placed from inventory.
    - Anchor slots can now be clicked to configure ingress and egress directly, consuming the matching anchor item from the player's inventory.
