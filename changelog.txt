Version: 0.1.4
Date: 2026-07-25
  Features:
    - Added a character-only bootstrap recipe for hand-grown pentapod eggs on Gleba, based on feedback from @Birk.
    - Added a character-only bootstrap recipe for hand-grown biter eggs on Nauvis.
  Changes:
    - Replaced Nauvis biter egg ingress and bioflux egress Managed Lines with Nauvis-only hand-crafted biter eggs.
    - Existing saves remove configured biter egg and bioflux Managed Lines; placed obsolete lines are not refunded.
  Bugfixes:
    - Base screenshots now capture the supported planet currently being viewed, use its current Square size, and include its name in the filename, based on feedback from @Birk.

Version: 0.1.3
Date: 2026-05-27
  Features:
    - Added Space Age support for planets, Vulcanus, Fulgora, Gleba and Aquilo.
    - Added a per-player "Get cliff explosives" mod setting button to help recover from cliff generation blocking progression on Vulcanus.
  Changes:
    - This is a major update, with entire systems reworked to better fit Space Age.
    - Reworked the ingress/egress system around craftable anchors.
    - Removed the expansion point purchasing system. Ingress and egress anchors are now crafted and placed from inventory.
    - Anchor slots can now be clicked to configure ingress and egress directly, consuming the matching anchor item from the player's inventory.
